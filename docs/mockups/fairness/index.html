Four ways to lay out the page

Same data, fonts and colours in each, so only the layout differs. Every one is clickable — open a person, switch tabs, try it on your phone.

  1. A
    Table and side panel

    Tabs per question. People to review is a plain table; clicking a person slides their detail in from the right while the list stays put.

    Good for: scanning everyone fast, then checking one at a time.

    →
  2. B
    Review queue

    One person at a time, full width, with Previous / Next, a progress bar and “Raise at calibration” or “Mark as checked”. Groups and managers move to a separate view.

    Good for: working through the list in a calibration meeting.

    →
  3. C
    One-page report

    No tabs. Opens with four plain findings, then the people (each opens in place), groups, managers and calibration moves, all on one scrolling page.

    Good for: someone who wants the whole story in one read, or to print.

    →
  4. D
    Chart first

    Everyone as one dot: record across, mark up. Dots far from the diagonal are the flagged people; click one to see why beside the chart.

    Good for: seeing the whole period’s pattern at a glance.

    →
