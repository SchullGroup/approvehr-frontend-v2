Fairness check

Whether marks in this period differ between groups of people, and by how much. A flag is a question to take into calibration, not a finding of bias.

HR only
Period H1 2026 appraisal · Calibration
Marks
People counted

Adjusting for objectives is off until managers confirm objective progress. Today the owner types their own progress figure, so adjusting for it would carry that bias into the comparison.

How this is worked out

What is compared
Each person's computed mark for the period, before calibration. Calibration moves are checked on their own, in the Calibration moves tab, so a move cannot hide a gap or create one unnoticed.
Each group against the rest
A group is compared with everyone else who has a mark in the same grouping. Where there are only two groups, they are compared with each other.
The range
The line behind each dot is where the group's true average probably sits (95% of the time). Fewer people means a wider line. A gap has a range worked out the same way.
When a gap is flagged
The gap is at least 3 points, its whole range sits on one side of zero, and both sides have at least 5 people. Every flag shows the numbers that triggered it.
Small groups
Groups of fewer than 5 people are hidden and named only with their size, so nobody can be picked out. This page never shows any one person's gender, age or other group.
Earlier periods
Adding the two previous periods makes small groups readable and ranges narrower. In this mockup the earlier periods are simulated.
Gender
Read from the free-text field: "F", "Female" and "Woman" count as women; "M", "Male" and "Man" as men. Anything else is left out of the gender comparison only, and counted so HR can fix the record.
Not checked yet
Religion and state of origin, until the NDPA 2023 review confirms a lawful basis and employees have been told. Marks adjusted for objectives, until managers confirm objective progress.
Managers
Flagged when their average is at least 5 points from everyone else's with the whole range on one side of zero, or when the spread of their marks is under 7 points, half the company's 14. Managers who rated fewer than 5 people are not shown.
