Fairness check

H1 2026 appraisal · Calibration
How flags work
  • Each group is compared with everyone else in the period, using marks before calibration.
  • A gap is flagged when it is at least 3 points and its likely range doesn't cross zero.
  • Groups under 5 people are hidden. Religion and state of origin aren't checked.
  • A manager is flagged at 5 points from everyone else, or when their marks barely vary.
