# Scenes

These illustrations are from **Open Doodles** by Pablo Stanley (https://www.opendoodles.com).

- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal, public domain. Free for commercial use, no credit required.
- Source: files from opendoodles.s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com, downloaded 2026-11-06 with the product owner's approval.
- Used: strolling, meditating, chilling, coffee, ice-cream.
- Changed: metadata, comments and ids removed and coordinates rounded to whole numbers to make the files
  smaller. The drawings are otherwise untouched.

They are decorative (`alt=""`) and are shown on a light sticker so the black line art reads in both themes.
